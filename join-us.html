FAQ

Common questions

Do I need a medical or healthcare background to join?
No. Most of our members are high school students with no healthcare experience. You'll learn about firefighter lung health as you go, and our Research & Medical Affairs team handles the science.
Are there a lot of volunteering opportunities?
Yes! Members can take part in hydration drives, firefighter appreciation events, awareness campaigns, fundraising, community outreach, and partnerships with local organizations.
How do I receive volunteer hours for an event?
Your chapter tracks attendance at every event. For verification forms or letters for school or service awards, ask your chapter president, or email projectpulmonary@gmail.com if you volunteered outside a chapter.
How can I become part of Project Pulmonary leadership?
Start or lead a chapter at your school, or apply for a national role: Executive Board, Graphic Designer, Research & Medical Affairs, Governmental Affairs, or Community Outreach. Each has its own application on the Get Involved page.
What if my school doesn't approve a chapter?
Reach out to us. We can share materials to help you make the case to your school, and talk through other ways to get involved in your community.
