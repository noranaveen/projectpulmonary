Our mission

We turn student leadership into direct support for firefighter lung health.

Our work connects schools, chapters, and communities with tangible service opportunities: hydration drives, firefighter appreciation projects, pulmonary health education, and awareness around long-term occupational exposure.

11K+ hydration items donated to fire stations nationwide
3K+ letters written to recognize and encourage firefighters
30+ student chapters expanding local service and education
What we do

Service programs designed to be clear, local, and easy for students to lead.

Each initiative is built to help young people educate their communities while supporting firefighters in practical, visible ways.

01
Hydration Drives

Students collect and deliver high-need supplies to local fire stations.

Chapters organize school-based drives, coordinate donations, and connect directly with fire departments.

02
Letters for Lungs

Classroom projects that turn appreciation into education.

Students write cards while learning why firefighter pulmonary health matters beyond the emergency scene.

03
Community Education

Youth-led presentations make occupational exposure easier to understand.

We help students explain chronic airborne carcinogen exposure through school outreach and community events.

Why it matters

Firefighters face risks that continue long after the smoke clears.

Repeated exposure to smoke, toxic particulates, diesel exhaust, and other airborne hazards can create long-term respiratory concerns. Project Pulmonary exists to make that issue more visible and to mobilize students around prevention, awareness, and support.
