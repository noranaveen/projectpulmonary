Protect Those
Who Protect Us.

Project Pulmonary is a 501(c)(3) public health nonprofit organization that focuses on lung cancer prevention in firefighters by providing pulmonary rehabilitation following carcinogenic inhalation of wildfire VOCs. Through hydration item donations, detoxification sauna installations, K-8 lung cancer screening letters, and student-led chapters, we turn awareness of long-term smoke exposure into direct, visible support.

Each program is designed to make pulmonary health tangible in schools and communities while directly supporting firefighters.

Hydration Drives
01

School-led drives that end in direct station donations.

Students coordinate hydration drives with local high schools, collect high-need supplies, and deliver them straight to nearby fire stations, a clear bridge between youth-led service and firefighter support.

Letters for Lungs
02

Classroom encouragement that reaches younger students.

K-8 students create handwritten letters and cards with lung cancer screening reminders for firefighters, learning about our mission while taking part in a simple, lasting act of encouragement and recognition.
