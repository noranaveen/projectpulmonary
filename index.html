Why it matters

The smoke firefighters breathe causes cancer.

Group 1

The WHO's cancer agency classified firefighting as carcinogenic to humans in 2022, alongside tobacco and asbestos.

75%

of firefighters honored at the IAFF's 2022 Fallen Fire Fighter Memorial died of occupational cancer, not injury.

~20%

of PAH exposure is absorbed through the skin, so turnout gear alone isn't enough.

Sources: IARC Monograph 132 (WHO, 2022) · IAFF

What we do

Three programs. Real supplies, in real stations.

01 · Hydration Drives

Water after the smoke.

Firefighting dehydrates crews fast. Chapters collect water and electrolytes and hand-deliver them to the station.

LA & Ventura CountyFDNY firehousesAlabama hydration tourDominican Republic
See the deliveries
02 · Letters for Lungs

Screening reminders, written by kids.

K–8 students write letters and posters that remind firefighters to get screened for lung cancer, delivered to crews and their families.

K–8 classroomsDelivered with every drive
Bring it to your school
03 · Detox Saunas

Helping crews decontaminate after the fire.

We fund detoxification sauna installations at fire stations, starting with $35,000 in grant funding already secured.
