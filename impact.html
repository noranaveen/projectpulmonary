How a delivery works

Every delivery follows three steps.

01

Collect

Chapters run school and community drives for water, electrolytes, and care items.

02

Deliver

Students hand supplies directly to the crew on shift, station by station.

03

Remind

Every delivery carries handwritten Letters for Lungs with a screening reminder.
