Our Impact

Measurable support, backed by real chapters, classrooms, and station visits.

Project Pulmonary's impact is both quantitative and visible: supplies delivered, letters written, chapters formed, and conversations started.

Hydration drives, station by station.

From LA County to FDNY firehouses to the Dominican Republic, Project Pulmonary shows up in person with water, gear, and gratitude for the crews on shift.

Sauna fundraising, underway

Actively raising support for firefighter detoxification sauna installations, building on grant funding already secured.

Letters for Lungs

Handwritten lung cancer screening awareness letters, written by students and delivered directly to firefighters and their families.

A national footprint

Chapters, hydration drives, and classroom visits spanning California, New York, Alabama, and beyond.

Growing every semester

New student-led chapters and fire station partnerships join the network as each school year begins.
