About us

Started after the Palisades Fire. Built by students.

In January 2025, the Palisades Fire tore through our Southern California community. We started Project Pulmonary for the crews who breathed it in.

Our founders

Two students, one conviction.

Co-Founder & CEO

Bettina Lee

The Palisades Fire pushed me to learn how overlooked the lung cancer risk from wildfire smoke really is.

Growing up in Southern California, wildfires were part of life. Bettina co-founded Project Pulmonary to combine public health with hands-on service, protecting firefighter health while bringing more students into the work.

Co-Founder

Elena

Catching disease early is often the difference between treatable and terminal.

Elena's grandmother was diagnosed with lung cancer only after it had spread. After watching firefighters breathe carcinogenic smoke during the Palisades Fire, she set out to protect them at every stage: prevention, care, and policy.
