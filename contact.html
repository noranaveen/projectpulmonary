FAQ

Common Questions

What is Project Pulmonary's mission?
Project Pulmonary supports firefighter lung health through youth-led service, advocacy, and community engagement, focused on crews facing chronic exposure to airborne carcinogens.
How are donations used?
Donations fund hydration drives, Letters for Lungs, chapter growth, and detoxification sauna installations that directly benefit local fire stations.
Do I need a chapter nearby to participate?
No. If there's no chapter in your area, you can apply to start one at your school.
