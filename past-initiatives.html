Kits4Candy

Kits4Candy provided firefighters with compact decontamination kits so they could begin removing soot and contaminants from skin and gear immediately after a call.

Trick or treat, but make it safe

Spooky Season, Serious Mission

This Halloween, our chapters traded candy runs for kit runs, packing decontamination kits for the crews who keep our neighborhoods safe all year.

Why Halloween?

Firefighters work through every holiday. A kit in the gear bag means soot comes off skin and gear before it can be absorbed or carried home, even on the busiest night of the year.

Why kits

Closing the Gap After the Call

Detox saunas help the body clear toxins, and our hydration drives support crews on the job. Neither one covers what stays behind on skin, gear, clothing and personal belongings once the call is over.

Kits4Candy addressed that gap. Each kit allows firefighters to clean exposed skin, isolate contaminated gear, and protect vehicle seats before contaminants can spread.

What’s inside

What Is in Each Kit

  • Clear zippered pouch
  • 4 carcinogen-removal wipes
  • 2 contamination bags
  • 2 pairs of nitrile gloves
  • Seat liner, when budget allows
  • Quick-reference card

Packed in a clear pouch so crews could see what’s inside and stations could check stock at a glance.

The quick-reference card

Right after the call

  1. Rinse or wipe exposed skin now: face, neck, hands.
  2. Bag your gear before it touches anything else.
  3. Shower within the hour.
  4. Wash gear separately from other laundry.
How it works

How It Works

01 · Fund

Funding

Kits were funded through grants and sponsors, pitched the same way chapters approach Hydration Drive sponsors. $45 covered a full engine crew.

02 · Build

Assembly

Volunteers assembled kits at chapter volunteer sessions, alongside Letters4Lungs, so a handwritten note could accompany each kit.

03 · Deliver

Delivery

Chapters coordinated with station leadership in advance and delivered kits in person, beginning with stations served through our hydration drives.

About this project

About the Campaign

This September, Project Pulmonary chapters across the country honored the 343 firefighters who lost their lives on September 11, 2001 by assembling and delivering hydration kits to local fire stations.

The campaign recognizes the sacrifices firefighters continue to make every day while promoting firefighter wellness and respiratory health.
