About this project

Honoring the 343 by Supporting Today’s Firefighters

This September, Project Pulmonary chapters across the country honored the 343 firefighters who lost their lives on September 11, 2001 by assembling and delivering hydration kits to local fire stations.

The campaign recognizes the sacrifices firefighters continue to make every day while promoting firefighter wellness and respiratory health.
