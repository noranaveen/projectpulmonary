Stocked for the Season

This holiday season, Project Pulmonary chapters are delivering festive stockings filled with Liquid I.V. hydration, hot cocoa, herbal tea, candy canes and nasal relief to fire stations across the country, each one paired with a handwritten Letters4Lungs thank-you.

Liquid I.V. Is Stocking the Stations

Liquid I.V., the #1 hydration brand in the United States, donated 60,000 hydration items to Project Pulmonary. Stocked for the Season is how those items reach the people they are meant for: firefighters at local stations.

Firefighters work long shifts in heavy gear through every holiday. Our chapters are making sure the crews in their own communities start the new year stocked up.

Inside every delivery

What’s in the Stocking

Every station gets real holiday stockings, packed with small things that help firefighters stay healthy through winter and spread a little holiday cheer.

  • Liquid I.V. packets

    Electrolyte hydration from our sponsor, for long shifts and busy nights.

  • Hot cocoa

    A warm cup between calls on cold winter nights.

  • Herbal tea

    Caffeine-free and soothing after a long, smoky shift.

  • Candy canes

    A little holiday cheer for the whole crew.

  • Nasal relief

    Nasal care products for dry winter air and smoke.

From Our Chapters to Their Stations

The same chapters that write letters and pack kits all year are bringing the holiday deliveries to their local crews.

Why the holidays?

Fire stations never close for the holidays, and Southern California’s Santa Ana wind season runs well into winter. A stocked station is one less thing for crews to worry about on the busiest nights of the year.
