Candy4Kits

Candy4Kits provides firefighters with compact decontamination kits, enabling them to begin removing soot and contaminants from skin and gear immediately after a call.

Why kits

Closing the Gap After the Call

Detox saunas help the body clear toxins, and our hydration drives support crews on the job. Neither one covers what stays behind on skin, gear, clothing and personal belongings once the call is over.

Candy4Kits addresses that gap. Each kit allows firefighters to clean exposed skin, isolate contaminated gear, and protect vehicle seats before contaminants can spread.

What’s inside

What Is in Each Kit

  • Clear zippered pouch
  • 4 carcinogen-removal wipes
  • 2 contamination bags
  • 2 pairs of nitrile gloves
  • Seat liner, when budget allows
  • Quick-reference card

Packed in a clear pouch so crews can see what’s inside and stations can check stock at a glance.

The quick-reference card

Right after the call

  1. Rinse or wipe exposed skin now: face, neck, hands.
  2. Bag your gear before it touches anything else.
  3. Shower within the hour.
  4. Wash gear separately from other laundry.
How it works

How It Works

01 · Fund

Funding

Kits are funded through grants and sponsors, pitched the same way chapters approach Hydration Drive sponsors. $45 covers a full engine crew.

02 · Build

Assembly

Volunteers assemble kits at chapter volunteer sessions, alongside Letters for Lungs, so a handwritten note can accompany each kit.

03 · Deliver

Delivery

Chapters coordinate with station leadership in advance and deliver kits in person, beginning with stations served through our hydration drives.
